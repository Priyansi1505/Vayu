Subject: Design a complete, detailed UI/UX for a long-scroll, data-driven air quality (AQI) monitoring and awareness website called "Vayu," spanning 11 distinct sections plus a detailed login flow and user dashboard.

Design Philosophy & Tone:
The design must be clean, transparent, and use friendly, modern text formatting (e.g., using approachable fonts like Poppins or Inter). The overall aesthetic should have a glassmorphism effect to create a sophisticated, lightweight, and modern feel, balanced by a "flowy like air" texture. Icons must be custom-designed small graphics that are clear, friendly, and symbolic of their function (e.g., a stylized "PM2.5 particle" rather than just a number; a tiny Indore landmark for the location).

1. Global Navigation (Top Sticky Bar)

Logo: The 'VAYU' logo on the far left.

Search Bar: Central, clean, with a magnifying glass icon and "Search location..." text.

Language Dropdown: Next to the search, showing a flag/code (e.g., EN) that opens to select other languages.

Theme Toggle Switch: A visible, intuitive toggle (e.g., sun/moon) to switch between Light and Dark modes.

Login/Signup: Buttons on the far right.

2. Hero Section (Indore Air Dashboard)

Layout: Based on image_0.png, using a dual-card glassmorphism layout against a sky background.

Dynamic Background Color: Crucial: The entire background or a large card within it must change color in real-time according to the AQI color scale (e.g., Green for Good, Yellow for Moderate, Red for Poor). This provides immediate visual context.

Left Card (Details): Displays the current AQI number (e.g., 40), the AQI status (e.g., "Good"), and small symbolic pollutant graphics (icons for PM2.5, SO2, etc.). The location 'Indore' is displayed.

Right Card (Map): A real-time, functional satellite/street map based on image_0.png. Crucial: The AQI color level must also be reflected on the map, with overlays or gradients that change with the AQI level and location.

3. Detailed Concentration Section

A section breaking down the specific concentration of major pollutants (PM2.5, PM10, NO2, SO2, CO, O3). Each pollutant must feature its symbolic little graphic. Below this, include the location/city name and a small graphic/text for current weather details (temp, humidity).

4. Predictive Graph & Motivation Section

Layout: Replicate the general feel of image_0.png but modernized.

Predictive Graph: A clean, dual-line graph showing AQI trends.

Clickable Chips (3 Options): At the top-right of the graph, replace previous selectors with clear, clickable "Chips" for selecting the timeframe: [Today], [3 Days], [Past Week]. Clicking a chip updates the graph.

Motivational Thought: A section below the graph with a small, stylized graphic and a motivational thought (e.g., "Protect your lungs today, for a healthier tomorrow.")

5. Moving Belts Section (Best & Worst Cities)

Concept: A highly visual, dynamic section showing two parallel, horizontal "moving belts" (like airport baggage carousels) moving continuously in opposite directions.

Belt 1 (Top): Shows cities with the BEST air quality, scrolling smoothly.

Belt 2 (Bottom): Shows cities with the WORST air quality, scrolling in the opposite direction.

Interaction: Clicking on any city card from either belt must cause that card to 'pop up' as a stylized glassmodal, displaying detailed stats for that city, pausing the belts momentarily, and overlaying the background.

6. News Updates Section (Moving Belts)

A section dedicated to air quality and environmental news.

Headline: "LATEST NEWS & UPDATES" at the bottom.

Moving Belts: A series of vertically oriented rectangular cards (like the cards in image_0.png, but stacked/scrolling). This belt also moves continuously. Clicking a card opens the full story.

7. Health Tips & Video (Interactive Section)

Concept: Cards for health tips/advice (e.g., "Avoid Outdoor Activity," "Use Air Purifiers").

Interaction: When a user clicks any tip card, it smoothly "comes forward" to the center, slightly enlarging, and plays an embedded short video related to that tip. Simultanenously, a strong blur effect is applied to the rest of the website background, focusing attention on the video.

8. AQI Importance & Chart

A clean text section explaining the importance of AQI data and why users should care.

AQI Chart: A visually clear, standardized AQI chart/legend (Green, Yellow, Orange, Red, Purple) showing the ranges and health implications of each level.

9. FAQs Section

A clean, accordion-style section. A list of frequently asked questions, each with a clickable "+" or arrow dropdown to reveal the answer (e.g., "What does AQI 150 mean?").

10. Contact & Data Resources

A section providing contact information.

Data Sources: A clear list, citing official government and reliable third-party environmental resources (e.g., CPCB India, OpenAQ) that provide the data.

11. Footer Section

Standard clean footer with sitemap, copyright information, and social media links.

12. Login/Signup Flow & User Dashboard (Post-Login)

Concept (Login/Signup): Based on the structure of image_1.png. It must use the same glassmorphism card and clean form inputs. Integrate the Social Login buttons (LinkedIn, Google) at the top. Use the "Create your account" text as the header and the clean green "CREATE AN ACCOUNT" button.

Concepts (User Dashboard): A complete dashboard interface presented with a glass background over a specific background image: an evening cityscape view with warm twilight colors.

Dashboard Features:

Allows users to "Select their desired cities" via a multi-select dropdown or search.

Displays stylized, smaller versions of the AQI dashboard (image_0.png) for all selected cities simultaneously, allowing for easy comparison at a glance.

Includes a notification settings section for personalized AQI alerts for their selected locations.
